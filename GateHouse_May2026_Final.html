Website & search reflect May (final complete month)
All website & search data filtered to US traffic only
May was the strongest all-around month of the year. Website traffic rebounded sharply — 861 views (+20.8% MoM) and 441 users (+60.9%) driven by the May 8 FHA+ send. The FHA+ send is now final at 202 unique opens (44.6% open rate) and 72 unique clicks (15.9% click rate), both records for the newsletter. LinkedIn content surged: 9 posts published drove 3K impressions (+103.5% MoM) with Brian Montgomery’s FHA appraisal flexibility post leading at 1,207 impressions and a 7.54% engagement rate. Search improved for the first time in two months — average position rose to 8.2 (from 8.9 in April), approaching the ≤8 target. One persistent flag: unsubscribes held at 11 (2.43%), above the <2% healthy threshold — review list composition before the June send. Bounce rate worsened to 68.8% MoM, partly attributable to FHA+ email traffic which tends to read one page and exit.

Definition Key
Views
Total page loads on the website, including repeat visits by the same user.
Total Users
Unique visitors to the site within the period — each person counted once.
Sessions
Distinct visits to the site. A session ends after 30 minutes of inactivity.
Bounce Rate
% of visits under 10 seconds with no meaningful interaction. Lower is better.
Impressions (Search)
Times the site appeared in Google search results, regardless of clicks.
Site CTR
Click-through rate — % of search impressions that resulted in a click to the site.
Avg. Position
Average search ranking across all queries. Lower = higher on results page. Target ≤8.
Impressions (LinkedIn)
Times a post appeared on any LinkedIn screen. Measures reach, not interaction.
Reactions
Emoji responses on LinkedIn posts — the most common engagement type.
Engagement Rate
Total interactions ÷ impressions. Measures how actively the audience engages.
Unique Opens
Individual subscribers who opened the FHA+ email at least once.
Unique Clicks
Individual subscribers who clicked at least one link in the email.
Hard Bounce
Email permanently undeliverable. Should be removed from the list.
Unsubscribe Rate
% of recipients who opted out. Healthy threshold is under 2% per send.
MoM / YoY
Month-over-month (vs. prior month) and year-over-year (vs. same period last year).
