Definition Key
Views
Total page loads on the website, including repeat visits by the same user.
Total Users
Unique visitors to the site within the period — each person counted once.
Sessions
Distinct visits to the site. A session ends after 30 minutes of inactivity.
Bounce Rate
% of visits under 10 seconds with no meaningful interaction. Lower is better.
Impressions (Search)
Times the site appeared in Google search results, regardless of clicks.
Site CTR
Click-through rate — % of search impressions that resulted in a click to the site.
Avg. Position
Average search ranking across all queries. Lower = higher on results page. Target ≤8.
Impressions (LinkedIn)
Times a post appeared on any LinkedIn member's screen. Measures reach, not interaction.
Reactions
Emoji responses (like, celebrate, etc.) on LinkedIn posts — the most common engagement type.
Engagement Rate
Total interactions ÷ impressions. Measures how actively an audience interacts with content.
Unique Opens
Individual subscribers who opened the FHA+ email at least once — each counted once.
Unique Clicks
Individual subscribers who clicked at least one link in the email — each counted once.
Hard Bounce
Email permanently undeliverable (invalid address). Should be removed from the list.
Unsubscribe Rate
% of recipients who opted out. Healthy threshold is under 2% per send.
MoM / YoY
Month-over-month (vs. prior month) and year-over-year (vs. same period last year).
